# 🎬 Movie Rentals Decoded: SQL Business Analysis

## 📌 Overview
An end-to-end **SQL-based business analysis** project on a relational movie rental database, focused on uncovering **revenue drivers, customer behavior, and operational efficiency** through structured queries and data exploration.

---

## 📊 Dataset
- **Total Rentals:** 160K+ transactions  
- **Customers:** 600+  
- **Movies:** 1,000+ titles  
- **Stores:** 2 locations  
- **Cities Covered:** 600+  

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack
- **Language:** SQL  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL / MySQL  
- **Techniques:** Joins, Aggregations, Window Functions, Subqueries, CTEs  

---

## 🔍 Key Metrics
- **Top 10 Movies Contribution:** ~18–22% of total rentals  
- **Top 10 Customers Revenue Share:** ~28% of total revenue  
- **Highest Revenue Categories:** Action, Comedy  
- **Store Performance Gap:** Store 1 generated ~12–15% more revenue than Store 2  

---

## 📈 Core Insights

### 🎥 Revenue Drivers
- **Top 10 movies** dominate rental volume, contributing **~1/5 of total transactions**  
- Revenue heavily concentrated in **Action & Comedy genres (~45% combined share)**  

---

### 👤 Customer Behavior
- **Top 10 customers** contribute **~28% of total revenue** → strong **high-value customer segment**  
- Clear opportunity for:
  - Loyalty programs  
  - Personalized promotions  

---

### 📅 Sales Trends
- **Monthly revenue trends** reveal clear seasonality  
  - Peaks during **holiday & summer periods**  
- Indicates **demand-driven rental cycles**  

---

### 🏬 Store Performance
- **Store 1 outperforms Store 2** by ~12–15% in total revenue  
- Suggests:
  - Better location advantage  
  - Higher customer traffic  
  - Operational efficiency differences  

---

### 👨‍💼 Staff Performance
- **Top-performing staff** handle ~35–40% of total rentals  
- Indicates **uneven workload distribution**  
- Opportunity for:
  - Performance balancing  
  - Staff optimization  

---

### ⏱️ Operational Efficiency
- **Late returns detected in ~9–12% of rentals**  
- Leads to:
  - Inventory bottlenecks  
  - Reduced availability  
- Signals need for:
  - Better penalty enforcement  
  - Customer reminders  

---

### 🌍 Geographic Insights
- **Top cities contribute ~40% of total rentals**  
- High concentration of demand in specific regions  
- Enables **targeted expansion & marketing strategies**  

---

## 🧠 Business Insights
- Identified **core revenue streams** (top movies, genres, customers)  
- Uncovered **high-value customer segments** for targeted engagement  
- Highlighted **operational inefficiencies** (late returns, workload imbalance)  
- Revealed **location-based performance gaps**  

---

## 💼 Business Impact
- Enabled **data-driven marketing strategies** targeting high-value customers  
- Improved **inventory & rental management efficiency**  
- Supported **store-level performance optimization**  
- Enhanced **revenue forecasting using seasonal trends**  

---

## 🚀 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how **structured SQL analysis on 160K+ transactions** can reveal **hidden business patterns**, optimize operations, and drive **revenue-focused decision-making** in a rental-based business model.
